🎥 Comment to Video

Tag @comment2video on your comment. If you’ve signed up, we’ll generate a video reply!

How it works

  1. Sign up with your account
  2. Tag @comment2video in your comment
  3. We produce a video inspired by your comment and the surrounding context of the video and comment thread, and post it as a draft to your account.
  4. If you say "@comment2video rn" then it is posted immediately
